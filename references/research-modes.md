# Research Modes

Choose the narrowest mode that answers the user's decision. Combine modes only when necessary.

## Market or sector landscape

Use for L1/L2, DeFi, stablecoins, RWA, DePIN, AI x Crypto, wallets, exchanges, custody, infrastructure, or a geographic market.

Minimum coverage:

- market definition and exclusions;
- demand drivers and user segments;
- market proxies with limitations rather than false-precision TAM;
- value chain and profit pools;
- leading projects and competitive clusters;
- adoption, capital, developer, and social signals;
- regulation and structural risks;
- scenarios and strategic implications.

## Protocol or token diligence

Use for a single network, protocol, DAO, or token.

Minimum coverage:

- problem and product;
- architecture and dependencies;
- users, activity, and distribution;
- business or fee model;
- token role and value accrual;
- supply, unlocks, concentration, and liquidity;
- governance and team;
- security record and audits;
- competition;
- catalysts, risks, and falsification indicators.

## Competitive comparison

Use a consistent comparison date and identical metrics. Explain material differences in data definitions. End with segment-specific conclusions rather than declaring one universal winner.

## Narrative and social-signal study

Define the observation window before searching. Compare attention with harder evidence such as usage, fees, liquidity, developer activity, or funding. Identify who benefits from the narrative and whether discussion is organic, coordinated, or dominated by official accounts.

## Recurring market brief

Compare each run with the prior baseline. Separate new facts, changed metrics, unchanged watch items, and invalidated theses. Do not repeat background unless it affects the current decision.
