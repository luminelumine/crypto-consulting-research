# Consulting Report Template

Use only sections relevant to the research mode. Keep the executive summary decision-oriented and the body evidence-led.

## Title page

- Subject and research question
- Intended audience
- Analysis date and data cut-off
- Research and educational-use disclaimer

## Executive summary

- Direct answer in 3–6 sentences
- Three most decision-relevant findings
- Principal risk or reason the thesis may fail
- Recommended next decision or validation step

## Scope and method

- Definitions and exclusions
- Time horizon and geography
- Sources and sampling approach
- Important data limitations

## Market context

- Market structure and relevant segment
- Current size or defensible proxies
- Growth and adoption indicators
- Regulatory and macro context

## Product, protocol, or ecosystem analysis

- User problem and value proposition
- Architecture and dependencies
- Traction and distribution
- Economic model and sustainability

## Token and on-chain analysis

- Token utility and value flows
- Supply, emissions, unlocks, and concentration
- Liquidity, trading venues, and market structure
- Usage, fees, revenue, and incentive dependence

## Competitive landscape

- Direct competitors and substitutes
- Consistent comparison table
- Differentiation, moat, switching costs, and gaps

## Narrative and community evidence

- Dominant narratives and their origin
- Repeated pains, objections, and praise
- Attention versus fundamental evidence
- Sampling limitations

## Scenarios

For each scenario include conditions, leading indicators, implications, and invalidation triggers. Avoid unsupported point forecasts.

## Risks and red flags

Order by potential impact and likelihood. Include evidence and mitigants; distinguish known issues from open questions.

## Conclusions and next steps

- What is known with high confidence
- What remains uncertain
- Decision implications by audience or segment
- Concrete next diligence actions

## Appendix

- Evidence ledger
- Calculations and definitions
- Data tables
- Source list
